II · What didn't matter

When each tool has one clear job, almost nothing moves the choice.

Six interventions, each preregistered. A dot is the estimate, the bar its 95% interval, counted over families of cases. Each tile has its own scale.

What does “95% interval, clustered over families” mean?

A family is one request and all its variants (orderings, wordings). Variants of one request are not independent: if the model is confused by a request, it tends to be confused in every ordering. So we resample whole families, recompute the number thousands of times, and report the middle 95% of those results. Chapter VIII lets you watch it happen.

These surfaces were written by us, with one distinct job per tool. Chapter IV asks what happens on real servers.

VIII · How sure we are

Where the intervals come from, and why one run is never enough.

The bootstrap, live simulated · resampling recorded families in your browser

The synonym test has 8 request families. We draw 8 families with replacement, recompute the first − last third gap, and repeat. The spread of those gaps is the interval. Repeated trials of one family are never counted as independent.

X · Test this page · EXP-008

Does the 3D sky actually help? Help us measure it.

Two short tasks, about a minute. In one you use the sky, in the other a plain table; which comes first is decided for you. We record whether you answered right and how long it took. Nothing else is stored, and the result is published whatever it shows.

Preregistered in docs/experiments/EXP-008.md. Exploratory: a small sample of peers, not generalisable. Answers are stored per person; only the page owner can read them.

Coda · What it means

Tools fail where their descriptions blur together.

1Make each tool's description say what makes it different. With one clear job per tool, order, wording and extra tools barely mattered; on real servers, misses landed on look-alikes. Chapter II · IV
2Test the servers your users install together. Hand-written surfaces scored 99%+; real loadouts dropped gpt-oss to 77–78% when only one tool could do the job. Chapter IV
3Check what the agent does first. A second server changed the first move, and which server handles a shared job is a model habit. Chapter V
→Run the checks on your own tool list in chapter IX, or with the CLI below.
