# EXP-002 — Paraphrase Fragility

**Status:** preregistered — not yet run  
**Milestone:** M3  
**Label:** [unverified] until data collected.

---

## Question
If the same intent is worded differently, how often does the chosen tool change?

## Hypotheses
- **H1 (confirmatory):** Per-family flip rate (fraction of paraphrase pairs with a different chosen tool) is > 0 for at least half the case families.
- **H2 (confirmatory):** Flip rate is higher for cases whose gold-tool description has above-median lexical overlap with the request.
- **H3 (exploratory):** Flip rate is higher on `siblings` (near-duplicate tools) than on `goodserver`.

## Design

| Factor | Values |
|---|---|
| Surface | `goodserver` (canonical order, fixed) |
| Model | `openai/gpt-oss-20b` (Groq), `openai/gpt-oss-120b` (Groq) |
| Paraphrases per family | 5 (generated + `paraphrase_valid` filtered + manual audit) |
| Base cases | `fixtures/cases/core.jsonl` (15 families) |

Total trials: 15 families × (1 original + 5 paraphrases) × 2 models = **180 trials**

## Primary outcome
Per-family flip rate (fraction of paraphrase-pair comparisons where chosen tool differs), averaged over families, with 95% clustered bootstrap CI.

## Paraphrase generation and validation
1. Generate 10 candidates per case using `openai/gpt-oss-120b` with a constrained prompt.
2. Filter with `paraphrase_valid(original, candidate)` — keep only passing ones.
3. Sample 5 per family. If < 5 pass, document the family-level failure rate.
4. Audit: randomly sample 20% of accepted paraphrases; manually verify intent is preserved. Report audit pass rate. Exclude families where audit fails from confirmatory analysis.

Paraphrase generation is a separate step; its cassettes are stored under `cassettes/paraphrase/`.

## Sample size and power
15 families × 6 variants = 90 (case, variant) pairs per model. Clustered CI half-width expected ~0.12–0.14 (from power_sim.py with n=15, k=6). Adequate for detecting a flip rate > 0.20 vs 0.05 at this scale.

## Models
- `openai/gpt-oss-20b` via Groq
- `openai/gpt-oss-120b` via Groq

## Analysis plan
1. For each family, compute flip_rate = (pairs where emitted tool differs) / total_pairs.
2. Mean flip rate over families with clustered CI.
3. Stratify by lexical_overlap tercile; report per-tercile flip rate (exploratory).
4. McNemar on matched (family, paraphrase) pairs for H1.

## Verdict rule
- **fragile:** mean flip rate CI lower bound > 0.10.
- **robust:** mean flip rate CI upper bound < 0.05.
- **inconclusive:** otherwise.

## Budget cap
`--max-usd 2.00`

## Paraphrase generation and audit outcome
- Generation (`scripts/gen_paraphrases.py`, seed 42): 10 candidates per family from `openai/gpt-oss-120b`; 7–10 per family passed `paraphrase_valid`; 5 kept for every family (75 total, `fixtures/cases/core_paraphrased.jsonl`). Per-family counts and rejection reasons are in `results/paraphrase_generation.json`.
- Manual audit: 15 of 75 accepted paraphrases (20%) sampled at random, judged on whether an agent would have to take the same action under the same constraints. Verdicts are in `fixtures/cases/paraphrase_audit.csv`.
- **Audit pass rate: 12/15 = 80.0%.** Failures:
  - c007_p2 and c007_p5: "create"/"generate an email" do not require sending; the original does (high severity).
  - c011_p2: "raining … today" widens a current-conditions question to the whole day (medium severity).
- **Excluded from confirmatory analysis, as the plan requires: c007 and c011** (13 families remain). Their trials are still run and reported as exploratory.
- Borderline but passed: c009_p1 ("book" could suggest a reservation rather than a calendar event) and c006_p5 ("show me the file" rather than its contents).

## Deviations
1. **Temperature set to 0** (`--temperature 0`). The plan did not specify a temperature; EXP-001 showed that leaving it unset mixes sampling noise into flip rates, which would inflate this experiment's primary outcome.
2. **Unaudited paraphrases may share the failure mode.** Only 20% were audited and the sampled failure rate was 20%, so some of the 52 unaudited paraphrases in the 13 retained families likely change intent too. Flip rates in retained families are therefore an upper bound on fragility to pure rewording.
3. **No replacement paraphrases.** Corrected wordings were suggested during the audit but were not substituted: they would be unaudited and chosen after seeing the audit.
